This Adelaide backyard needed practical outdoor storage that would fit a tight side space and sit cleanly on an existing concrete slab. NB Fixmate assembled and installed a metal garden shed from kit components through to a finished, ready-to-use structure.

The job included laying out the base, building braced door panels, cutting and fitting metal framing, and completing the roof, vents, and double doors. The finished shed gives the property secure, weather-ready storage without crowding the lawn or walkway.
